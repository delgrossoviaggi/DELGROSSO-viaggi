Base: V184 PAYMENT RECOVERY HARDENED

V185 adds a mandatory post-recovery verification step in Archivio:
- booking confirmation recovery runs the existing document-generation/archive flow;
- payment receipt recovery runs the existing receipt/archive flow;
- after the service reports success, Archivio reloads the authoritative archive dataset;
- the row must then have a non-empty storage path before the UI can show “Archiviato ✓”;
- if the path is still missing, the operation is reported as not completed rather than showing a false success.

Safety:
- no new booking is created;
- no booking/payment amounts are changed;
- no automatic email is sent by recovery;
- no mass historical repair is performed automatically;
- no destructive database operation is included.
