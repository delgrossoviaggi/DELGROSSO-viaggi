# DEL GROSSO GESTIONALE V100 — ADVANCED EXECUTIVE

V100 is the next major visual/operational layer after V99.

## Added
- Executive Operations 360 dashboard panel.
- Live one-shot aggregation of trips, bookings, fleet and clients.
- Exact monetary presentation to 2 decimals for executive figures.
- Outstanding-payment intelligence.
- Capacity/occupancy pulse.
- Fleet availability pulse.
- Operational priority cards.
- End-to-end business workflow visualization.
- Quick links to critical modules.
- Responsive desktop/tablet/mobile presentation.
- No global polling and no global MutationObserver.

## Compatibility
- Existing Supabase core preserved.
- Existing local authentication preserved.
- V98 monetary precision retained.
