V182 — ARCHIVE RECOVERY

Added an operator-safe recovery action to Archivio documenti for booking confirmations without confirmation_storage_path.
The action retrieves the authoritative booking/trip context, regenerates the confirmation PDF, archives it through send-booking-confirmation with sendEmail=false, then refreshes the archive.
No booking is created or duplicated. No database schema/data migration is performed.
Also added a live count of documents still missing storage_path and bumped Service Worker cache to V182.

Static validation: JS syntax checked; no local asset deletion.
